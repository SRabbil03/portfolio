JUST Journalists Association

Official web platform of the Jashore University of Science and Technology Journalists' Association (যবিপ্রবি সাংবাদিক সমিতি)

● In Development

A full-stack platform where association members publish news and features, generate shareable photo cards, and manage the association's gallery, magazine, and member records, all behind role-based access control. Built as my Web Engineering Laboratory project.

  • Three-level roles: Member, Admin and Super Admin, each with its own dashboard
  • Invitation-code registration with email OTP, secure password setup and Super Admin–managed password recovery
  • News and feature publishing with a rich-text editor, drafts and preview
  • Photo card generator for news in feed, portrait and landscape layouts
  • Photo gallery with public/private visibility and monthly magazine uploads
  • Member directory, committee and About pages, Unicode–Bijoy converter and light/dark theme
